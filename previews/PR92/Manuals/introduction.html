Introduction ​

Transport maps describe a complicated probability distribution as a deterministic transformation of a simpler one. This provides a common framework for sampling, density estimation, and Bayesian inference [3]. This introduction adapts that framework to the notation and workflows used in TransportMaps.jl; [5] provides a more extensive tutorial.

For this direction, evaluate(M, reference_samples) produces approximate target samples. No posterior training samples are required. The Bayesian BOD example demonstrates the full workflow; [6] discusses transport-based Bayesian inference in structural health monitoring.
